The problem

Why supplier shortlists stall

Good suppliers are hard to find.

Buyers go back to the names they already know, so capable small businesses never get a look.

Bids arrive in different shapes.

Informal descriptions, mixed languages and scanned paperwork make a side-by-side comparison slow and subjective.

Trust is a guess until the first invoice.

Without current documents or a track record, a new supplier is a risk nobody wants to sign off on.

How it works

From unknown supplier to a shortlist you can defend.

01

Suppliers build a profile.

They upload CIPC, tax and B-BBEE documents. Each one becomes a badge that expires with the document and prompts a renewal.

02

Bids meet your requirements.

Vula Flow extracts the fields, checks their formats and scores each bid against the requirements your team sets.

03

You compare and decide.

Bids sit side by side with every field traced to its source, so the shortlist is something you can explain to your committee.

Built for trust

The AI reads. People verify.

Paperwork is messy, and a wrong guess can cost a contract. So the system flags what it is unsure about instead of filling the gap.

  • Blurry scans, stamps over text and handwriting go to a reviewer.
  • A file labelled as a CIPC document that is really a B-BBEE certificate gets caught.
  • Nothing is stored until the extracted value is found in the source text.
