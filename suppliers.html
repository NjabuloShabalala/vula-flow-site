How it works

Three steps. No pile of paperwork.

01

Add your documents.

Take a photo or upload a PDF of your CIPC papers, tax PIN and B-BBEE certificate.

02

We read them. A person checks.

VulaFlow pulls out the details. If it is not sure, a reviewer looks at the document. You see what was found and can correct it.

03

Buyers find you.

Your profile is matched to what corporate buyers are looking for. Every current document earns a badge buyers can trust.

Questions

Before you start

What do I need?

Your company registration papers, your tax compliance PIN and your B-BBEE certificate, if you have one. A phone camera is enough.

What if I do not have a B-BBEE certificate?

You can still build a profile. Buyers will see which documents are missing, and you can add them when you have them.

Does it cost anything?

Building your profile is free.
